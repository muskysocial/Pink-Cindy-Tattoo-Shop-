Meet the artist

Art with roots, movement and meaning.

My story begins in Los Angeles, where I was born and raised, with Chinese heritage that is part of who I am. Tattooing gives me a way to bring cultures, memories and personal stories together through art.

I love working with people to turn an idea into something they can carry with them — whether it is a detailed sleeve, a mythological scene, a cover-up plan or a tiny symbol that means the world. I work by appointment in my Los Angeles shop, offer selected home-service appointments, and travel for bookings when schedules allow.
